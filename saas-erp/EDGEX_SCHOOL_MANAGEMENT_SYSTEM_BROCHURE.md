## 🌟 Executive Introduction

**EdgeX School Management System (EdgeX SMS)** is a next-generation, all-in-one Institutional ERP designed to automate 100% of school operations. Engineered for extreme speed, rich visual aesthetics, and effortless usability, EdgeX SMS empowers School Owners, Directors, Principals, Accountants, Coordinators, and Teachers to run their campuses with zero paperwork, zero fee leakage, and complete peace of mind.

Unlike conventional school software that is slow, cluttered, expensive, and difficult for non-technical staff to operate, **EdgeX SMS** delivers an ultra-fast, modern interface with a customizable **High-Density Compact Mode** that allows staff to view maximum information without endless scrolling—all while remaining remarkably cost-effective and light on school budgets.

### SECTION 1: 🎓 Student Admission, Records & Lifecycle Management
* **6-Step Detailed Student Registration**: Comprehensive profiles capturing student bio, NADRA B-Form / CNIC, date of birth, blood group, medical cautions, emergency doctor details, and previous school history.
* **Customizable Admission Form**: Add custom school-specific fields (e.g. House, Transport Route, Quota, Concession Category) to the admission form with 1 click.
* **Batch Enrollment & Excel Import**: Onboard hundreds of students simultaneously using smart Excel/CSV file imports.
* **Session-End Student Promotion Engine**: 1-Click batch promotion of entire classes to the next academic session with pass/fail filtering.
* **360° Comprehensive Student Profile**: Unified timeline showing student attendance history, fee payment ledgers, exam performance, documents, and sibling connections.
* **Custom Student Register & List Builder**: Dynamic column chooser to create custom printable student lists filtered by gender, class, status, or admission date.
* **Official Institutional Document Generator**:
  * 📜 **School Leaving Certificate (SLC / Transfer Certificate)** with counter-signature formatting.
  * 📜 **Character Certificate** with formal institutional endorsements.
  * 📜 **Birth Certificate & Progress Certificates**.

---

### SECTION 2: 💳 Fee Management, Invoicing & Financial Accounting
* **3-Part & 4-Part Bank Challan Customizer**: Generates professional bank deposit vouchers (Bank Copy, School Copy, Parent Copy, Accounts Copy) with school logo, bank branch account numbers, barcodes, and late fee surcharge terms.
* **Rapid EasyFee Counter**: High-speed single-student fee collection screen designed for busy fee counters, printing thermal/slip receipts in under 3 seconds.
* **1-Click Monthly Batch Invoicing**: Generate monthly invoices for the entire school or individual classes with automated base tuition, exam funds, paper funds, and transport charges.
* **Class-Wise 12-Month Fee Matrix**: Full-year cross-tab register showing exact amounts paid by each student month-by-month, with class and school-wide grand totals.
* **Arrears & Overdue Aging Analysis**: Automatic categorization of unpaid dues into aging brackets (1 month, 2-3 months, 3-6 months, 6+ months) for targeted collection recovery.
* **Sibling & Scholarship Concession Policies**: Automatic calculation of fee discounts for 2nd/3rd siblings, staff children, or merit-based scholarships.
* **Advance Fee & Bulk Discount Entry**: Handle advance fee payments across future terms and batch apply fee concessions.
* **Fine Policy Engine**: Automated configurable late fee penalties (flat fee or per-day fine) applied after due date with admin waiver controls.
* **Family Groups & Sibling Linking**: Automatically links siblings by Father CNIC/Mobile to view consolidated family billing on a single screen.

### SECTION 3: 💰 Complete Income & Expense Management
* **Daily Expense Entry Vouchers**: Record daily operational school expenditures with expense title, category, payment source, invoice reference, and receipt attachments.
* **Customizable Expense Heads**: Manage operational categories (e.g. Utility Bills, Generator Fuel, Building Rent, Office Stationery, Teacher Tea/Refreshments, Maintenance & Repairs, Lab Consumables).
* **Payment Sources & Petty Cash**: Track expenditure across Cash-in-Hand, Principal Petty Cash, and multiple Bank Accounts.
* **Budget Planning & Limit Tracking**: Set monthly/annual departmental budget caps with real-time actual vs. budget variance indicators.
* **Profit & Loss / Income-Expense Statement**: Real-time financial summary displaying Total Fee Inflow vs Total Outflow, Net Surplus/Deficit, and financial health trends.
* **Institutional General Ledger**: Complete double-entry Chart of Accounts, Journal Vouchers, and Trial Balance.

### SECTION 4: 📷 QR Code Attendance Kiosk & Digital ID Card Generation
* **Live Camera QR Attendance Scanner**: Transform any laptop, tablet, or smartphone camera into an ultra-fast check-in/check-out kiosk for **both Students and Staff** with instant audio feedback.
* **Batch QR Attendance Card Generator**: 1-Click generation of durable QR attendance cards for all students and staff.
* **Automated Student Digital ID Card Generator**:
  * Auto-generates ready-to-print ID cards with Student Photo, Class, Roll No, B-Form, Blood Group, Father Contact, and Barcode/QR.
  * Supports Portrait and Landscape dimensions.
* **Staff Digital ID Card Generator**: Professional employee ID cards with Staff Role, Department, Joining Date, CNIC, and Emergency Contact.
* **ID Card Template Designer**: Customize background themes, institutional logos, authorized signatures, color accents, and card headers.
* **Daily & Monthly Attendance Reports**: Real-time absent student lists with 1-click WhatsApp absent alert dispatch to parents.
* **31-Day Official Inspection Attendance Register (خاضری رجسٹر)**: Standard cross-tab grid format (Present, Absent, Leave, Holiday) ready for official departmental inspections.

### SECTION 5: 📚 Academic Management, Timetables & Daily Lesson Planning
* **Classes, Sections & Subjects Manager**: Multi-section structure with compulsory and elective subject mappings.
* **Visual Interactive Timetable Grid**: Master school timetable view, class timetable view, and individual teacher schedules.
* **AI-Assisted Auto-Timetable Scheduler**: Automatically generates clash-free weekly timetables across all classes, subjects, and teachers in seconds.
* **Teacher Substitution & Period Arrangement Engine**:
  * Detects absent teachers from morning attendance automatically.
  * Smart algorithm finds available teachers who have a **FREE period** at that exact slot.
  * 1-Click **Auto-Assign** distributes substitutions evenly across free staff.
  * Prints **Daily Master Noticeboard Sheets** and **4-Up Teacher Slips (ارینجمنٹ پرچی)**.
  * 1-Click WhatsApp notification sent directly to substitute teachers.
* **Daily Teacher Diary**: Digital homework, classwork, and teacher remarks broadcasted daily to parents without paper diaries.
* **Weekly, 15-Day & Monthly Lesson Planner**: Curriculum planning module for teachers to outline SLOs, chapter objectives, activities, and tests, complete with Class Incharge review and landscape printouts.
* **Teacher Evaluation & Recognition**: Tracks teacher KPIs, student feedback, and automated *Teacher of the Month* leaderboards.

* **Examination Roll Number Slips / Admit Cards**: Printable exam admit cards featuring student photos, roll numbers, exam dates, subject schedules, and center rules.
* **Customizable Multi-Template Report Cards**:
  * Branded terminal progress cards with subject-wise marks, class averages, grade scales, attendance percentage, and teacher/principal signatures.
  * Report Card Template Designer to customize layout, colors, and branding elements.
* **Teacher-Wise & Subject-Wise Result Analytics**: In-depth academic quality audit measuring average class scores and teacher effectiveness.

### SECTION 7: 🚪 Front Desk, Campus Security & Gate Pass Management
* **Student Early Departure Gate Pass**:
  * Enforces campus security when students are collected before dismissal time.

  * Authorized by Principal / Admin.
* **Visitor & Vendor Entry Book**: Logs all campus visitors, time-in, time-out, and purpose of visit.
* **Dual Print Formats**: Prints on standard A5 paper or 80mm/58mm thermal gate receipt printers for security guards.
* **Instant Parent WhatsApp Alert**: 1-Click WhatsApp confirmation sent to parents confirming their child has left school premises.
* **Daily Gate Pass Audit Register**: Searchable history of all student departures and visitor logs.
* **Admission Inquiries & CRM Pipeline**: Track walk-in admission inquiries from initial visit, assessment test scheduling, test clearing, to final admission enrollment.
